# Phase 49 — Professional Business Day Language

- Production UI wording cleaned for Open Business Day and Close Business Day.
- Removed non-professional mixed-language strings from client-facing day close modal, topbar, banners, and reports notes.
- Business day labels now use: `Business Day <number> · <date> · <status>`.
- Close Day guidance now says: complete, pay, or void all open bills before closing the business day.
- Cash drawer wording remains clear: Physical Cash Count is cash drawer only; Card/Online are recorded separately in tender reports.
- Online-only mode preserved; offline modules remain removed.
