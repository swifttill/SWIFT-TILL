# Phase 48 — Tender-wise Day Close Reports

Purpose: clarify Day Open/Close cash handling and ensure card/online tender sales are visible in closeout and reports.

Rules:

- Opening Cash Float = physical till cash placed in the drawer at day start.
- Physical Cash Count = actual cash counted in the drawer at Day Close.
- Cash drawer expected amount = Opening Cash Float + Cash Sales - Cash Refunds.
- Cash difference = Physical Cash Count - Expected Drawer Cash.
- Card and Online payment selections are recorded in Tender Summary and Payment Summary.
- Card/Online are not added to physical drawer cash.
- Reports show Cash, Card, Online tender sales/refunds/net separately.
- Offline mode stays removed; no offline billing/sync modules are reintroduced.
